BouncyCastle.Cryptography 2.7.0
https://www.bouncycastle.org/
https://github.com/bcgit/bc-csharp/tree/release-2.7.0

Used as the managed ChaCha20-Poly1305 implementation when the operating system
does not provide .NET's implementation. VIIPER authentication and its encrypted
v2 wire protocol are unchanged; there is no plaintext or v1 fallback.
