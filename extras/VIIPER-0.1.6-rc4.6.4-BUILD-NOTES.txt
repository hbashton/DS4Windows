Validation and scope:

The complete tagged workflow succeeded. The Windows/amd64 job checked fresh
licenses, clean VCS, Release metadata, PE versions and a help-only smoke test.
Downstream inspection matched the executable's Go build metadata to the receipt
without launching the broker. Publication requires the release archive to match
this verified tagged build and the corresponding-source archive hash above.

This revision adds an explicitly negotiated Sony Bluetooth WDL sinc converter.
DS4Windows requests it only for a verified physical Bluetooth DualSense or
DualSense Edge media target; legacy callers and Nintendo media targets retain
their existing conversion. The fixed-rate Go port is an altered derivative,
not the original WDL implementation. Its upstream revision, attribution and
license are retained in VIIPER-WDL-NOTICE.txt and the corresponding source.

The ISO-OUT scheduler retains its absolute sample timeline through a late
completion rather than shifting already-consumed audio and every later window.
A fully expired start window still reanchors to bound catch-up. Cancellation,
stream reset and input endpoint scheduling remain independently fenced.

The broker revision does not include the private native-output recorder used
during diagnosis. It does not infer game ownership from foreground processes,
change controller gain, or replace DS4Windows's native-command/PCM arbitration.
Automated and package validation do not promise identical effects in every game.

The kong-yaml dependency remains pinned to a56dc1466c79ffc467aac6e158b878b2d7903bd2
with its MIT COPYING grant. The aggregate, GPL, systray and WDL notices preserve
the exact bytes shipped by the tagged CI artifact.
