## Source interpretation and existing coverage

- In both historical `v4.0.2.3` and current `ControlService`, permanent output initialization precedes physical discovery. Therefore the first two minimal logs do not alone pinpoint a HID or virtual-output API. Current startup can also perform other setup before discovery; the 19-second boundary is not proof that one specific HID call consumed the whole interval.
- `DS4Devices.findControllers` logs Found Controller only after enumeration, candidate filtering/opening, serial lookup, `PrepareDS4Init`, `PostInit`, and `PostDS4Init`. For an ordinary DS4, `DS4Device.PostInit` includes calibration and initial effect output. Current `HidDevice.ReadSerial` uses a synchronous native serial/feature query; Bluetooth `RefreshCalibration` can make up to five synchronous feature reads. Exclusive opening also has a device re-enable fallback. These are candidates within the uninstrumented boundary, not established causes in the attached logs.
- Current `OutContTypeCompatibility.Normalize` maps old X360/DS4 settings to VIIPER. The July ViGEm plugin error is not a current-runtime error to repair by reintroducing or resetting ViGEm.
- The already-implemented [#83 correction](https://github.com/hbashton/DS4Windows/issues/83) prevents active USB/Bluetooth Steam Input reclaim restarts. It removes the Bluetooth restart mechanism implicated by the later reclaim-timeout line, but does not prove the earlier pre-admission delay fixed.
- The already-implemented [#84 correction](https://github.com/hbashton/DS4Windows/issues/84) restores the control pipe for ordinary Bluetooth DS4 effect reports while preserving the separate audio writer. It addresses a source-backed mechanism compatible with physical write failures; `LastErrorCode=5` alone does not establish that it caused this reporter's failure, and does not prove a faulty controller or Bluetooth adapter.
- Repeated speaker-control failures show unsuccessful audio activation. Their occurrence after association cannot explain the preceding 19 seconds. Current audio/transport ownership has changed substantially since RC4.3; no new audio rewrite is justified by that old sequence alone.

## Decision and useful next evidence

**No speculative production fix is proposed for #32.** Existing fixes address narrower, demonstrable mechanisms, not every symptom in this multi-version report.

If the delay remains on a build containing the current fixes, collect a fresh full log with **Verbose Startup Logging** enabled. Existing `AssignInitialDevices begin/end`, `DS4Devices.findControllers dispatch begin/end`, and per-controller/output markers distinguish permanent-output setup from discovery and later association. Also record whether the controller was already awake/connected when startup began, the selected output mode, and whether this was logon startup or a manual launch. The posted attachments contain no `[StartupDiag]` markers, despite the earlier request to enable them.

If a new trace brackets the delay inside discovery, the next bounded development step is opt-in cold-start phase timing around enumeration, open/re-enable, serial lookup, calibration, and initial output—not a guessed timeout, retry loop, or forced disconnect. A hang dump captured while the delay is actually present can identify the blocked managed/native stack; review its private contents before sharing it. Current logs do not prove a hardware fault, the exact blocking API, or that more threading would solve the problem.
