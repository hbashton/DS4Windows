Behavioral scope:

This revision removes VIIPER's built-in automatic release checks, update
notifications and self-update execution. The legacy update-notify flag,
environment variable and configuration keys still parse for compatibility but
are ignored and normalized to none. Existing dismissal state is not deleted.
Normal server, startup registration, tray, explicit install/uninstall and the
separate DS4Windows installer/repair workflow remain available. Unused updater
dialog dependencies are removed; the locally vendored tray derivative and its
complete Apache notice remain.

The previously shipped, explicitly negotiated Sony Bluetooth WDL conversion and
ISO-OUT clock behavior remain unchanged in this revision. Legacy and Nintendo
targets retain their prior conversion. The altered Go resampler's pinned WDL
origin and complete license remain in VIIPER-WDL-NOTICE.txt and source. No
private capture/trace recorder or controller gain changes are included.

The kong-yaml dependency remains pinned to a56dc1466c79ffc467aac6e158b878b2d7903bd2
with its MIT COPYING grant. Aggregate, GPL, systray and WDL notices preserve the
exact bytes of the tagged Windows CI archive. Automated and package validation
do not promise identical controller effects in every game.
